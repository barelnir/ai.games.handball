Proportions

The head is about half the hero's height. Bodies are short and round, with stubby arms and legs and big shoes. Pivots and keepers are wider, and wings are smaller and quicker.

Ink style

Every shape gets a dark outline that stays the same thickness at any zoom, so heroes stay readable even when they're tiny on a phone.

Court

A bright blue court with orange goal areas, thick white lines, a ball logo in the centre, advertising boards and a bouncing cartoon crowd.

Powers ready

Each hero's look matches a planned power: Zip's bolt for a speed dash, Coil's coils for longer reach. These connect to the power slot that's already in the game.
