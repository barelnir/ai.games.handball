The rules

  • Orange zone: keepers only. Jump in to shoot.
  • 3 steps, 3 seconds with the ball. Bounce it to keep running.
  • Steal from the front. From behind is a foul.
  • Foul = free throw. Big chance fouled = 7-metre shot.
  • Cards: yellow, then 2 minutes off, then red.
  • No stalling: after the warning, 4 passes then shoot.
