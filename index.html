How handball works

The court

40 × 20 m with a 3 × 2 m goal at each end. The solid 6-metre line marks the goal area: only the goalkeeper may stand in it. Attackers may jump in, but must release the ball before landing. The dashed line is the 9-metre free-throw line, and the short mark at 7 m is the penalty spot.

Teams & time

Each side has 6 court players and a goalkeeper. A match is two halves of 30 minutes (shortened here) and teams change ends at half-time. After every goal the conceding team restarts with a throw-off from the centre.

Carrying the ball

  • 3 steps – you may take at most three steps while holding the ball.
  • 3 seconds – you may hold it for at most three seconds.
  • Dribble as long as you like, but once you catch it again you can't start a new dribble (double dribble).

Breaking these gives the other team a free throw.

Defending

Use your body from the front to block. Pulling, pushing or hitting – especially from behind – is a foul and gives a free throw. If the foul destroys a clear chance to score, it's a 7-metre throw: shooter against keeper.

Punishments

Repeated or dangerous fouls bring a yellow card, then a 2-minute suspension (the team plays one short). A third suspension is a red card.

Passive play

A team can't keep the ball without trying to score. After the referee's raised-arm warning the attack has 4 passes left before it must shoot.

Restarts

Throw-in when the ball crosses a sideline. Goalkeeper throw when the attackers put it over the goal line (or the keeper saves it out). Corner throw-in when a defender last touched it.
